# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be respectful, be direct, don't be a jerk. This applies in issues, PRs,
discussions, and any other project space.

## Expected

- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, but own the impact of what you say.
- Keep disagreements about code and decisions, not people.

## Not okay

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their consent.
- Any conduct that would reasonably make a contributor feel unwelcome.

## Enforcement

Report a violation by opening an issue or contacting a maintainer directly.
Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that
don't align with this Code of Conduct, and may warn or ban anyone for
behavior they deem inappropriate.

This applies to project spaces (issues, PRs, discussions) and to public
spaces when someone is representing the project.
